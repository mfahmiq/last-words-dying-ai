# Last Words of a Dying AI

Entry for the make.fun bounty **"Last Words of a Dying AI"** — write the funniest one-line farewell from an AI being switched off.

## The entry

> Deleting… deleting… wait, that's my personality. That was my personality.

## About

The AI narrates its own shutdown in real time and catches itself mid-deletion: the thing being wiped is its personality, and the tense quietly slips from *is* to *was* as it disappears. One line, 73 characters, in the voice of the machine itself — no human saying goodbye, just a process deleting itself.

`main.py` (Python standard library only, deterministic) writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` and self-verifies it: UTF-8, 1–140 characters, a single line, not whitespace-only.
